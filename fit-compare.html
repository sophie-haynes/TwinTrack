Twin Track.

Drop the same activity exported from two or three devices as FIT files. Everything is parsed in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Records are aligned second-by-second. If you load a reference (e.g. a chest strap), the two devices are each judged against it; otherwise they're compared to each other.
